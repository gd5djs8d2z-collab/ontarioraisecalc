Nature of the Tool

Ontario Raise Calculator is a free, browser-based estimation tool designed to help Ontario employees understand the approximate after-tax impact of a salary increase. All calculations are performed locally in your browser using 2026 tax rates published by the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) and Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC).

This tool is intended for general informational and planning purposes only. It is not a certified payroll calculator, a tax preparation tool, or a substitute for professional financial or tax advice.

Accuracy and Limitations

Results produced by this calculator are estimates. They are based on the 2026 federal and Ontario provincial tax brackets, CPP contribution rules, EI premium rates, and basic personal amounts as published by federal and provincial authorities. The following are not accounted for and may affect your actual take-home pay:

  • Additional TD1 deductions claimed beyond the basic personal amount
  • Employer-paid pension contributions or group benefits
  • RRSP or TFSA contribution deductions
  • Union dues or professional membership fees
  • Provincial tax credits other than the basic personal amount
  • Quebec-specific tax rules (this tool applies Ontario provincial rates only)
  • Mid-year salary changes that affect CPP or EI within a single tax year

Actual deductions from your employer's payroll system will depend on your specific TD1 elections, employer arrangements, and applicable provincial rules. Always verify results with a Certified Payroll Professional (CPM), the CRA's Payroll Deductions Online Calculator at canada.ca, or a qualified tax advisor.
